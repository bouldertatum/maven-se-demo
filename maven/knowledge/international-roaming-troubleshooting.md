# International Roaming Troubleshooting

Use this sequence when an authenticated customer has poor international wireless service:

1. Confirm the destination country, affected line, and whether voice, text, or data is failing.
2. Verify that international roaming is enabled for the line and compatible with the plan.
3. Review the current visited network and network-registration state.
4. If registration is stale, a network-registration refresh is a reversible recovery action. Explain it and obtain confirmation first.
5. After refresh, the customer may need to enable data roaming and restart or toggle airplane mode on the device.
6. Do not reset network settings unless the customer understands that saved Wi-Fi and related device settings may be removed.
7. If no compatible network is available or the refresh fails, escalate with the country, device, plan, enrollment, current network, and attempted steps.

This is synthetic demonstration guidance and is not an official carrier procedure.
