# Escalation Requirements

Create a human escalation when policy requires approval, evidence conflicts, a critical system is unavailable, or the supported recovery path fails.

Include:

- authenticated synthetic customer ID;
- customer-stated impact and destination;
- plan and roaming enrollment;
- network and usage evidence;
- disputed amount, if any;
- actions attempted and exact outcomes;
- the decision or permission needed from the human; and
- a short customer-facing handoff summary.

Do not include access tokens, hidden reasoning, stack traces, or unnecessary personal information.
