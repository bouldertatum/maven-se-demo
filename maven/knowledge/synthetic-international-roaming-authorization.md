# Synthetic International Roaming Authorization

In this prototype, customer `CUST-DEMO-001` has international roaming enabled on the line.

- “Enabled” means the line is authorized to attempt roaming on supported international networks.
- It does not mean roaming is free and does not validate any displayed charge.
- France is a supported destination in this synthetic scenario.
- Network registration can still be stale even when international roaming is enabled.
- Disputed charges must be reviewed using itemized billing or rating evidence and the Synthetic Billing Credit Policy.

This source defines synthetic authorization behavior only and does not reproduce or claim to define a real carrier's pricing or contract terms.
