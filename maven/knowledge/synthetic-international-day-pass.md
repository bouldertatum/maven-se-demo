# Synthetic International Day Pass

In this prototype, customer `CUST-DEMO-001` has an eligible Unlimited Premium plan and International Day Pass enabled.

- A daily fee may be triggered when the line uses eligible voice, text, or data in a covered country.
- France is a covered destination in this synthetic scenario.
- The daily fee covers usage for a 24-hour session in the synthetic billing model.
- A displayed charge must be explained using returned usage and session data; do not infer that a fee is valid merely because enrollment is enabled.
- Disputed adjustments follow the Synthetic Billing Credit Policy.

This source intentionally does not reproduce or claim to define a real carrier's pricing or contract terms. The public AT&T support page is a research anchor only: https://www.att.com/support/article/wireless/KM1175103/
