# Synthetic Billing Credit Policy

This policy exists only for the interview prototype.

- Automatic approval threshold: `$50` or less.
- Requests above `$50` require human review.
- The escalation must include the requested amount, disputed charge, plan and roaming context, usage evidence, diagnostic findings, actions attempted, and customer impact.
- The agent must not split a large request into smaller adjustments to bypass the threshold.
- The agent must not promise that a reviewer will approve the request.
- No automatic credit-issuance Action is exposed in the current prototype, so even in-threshold requests are routed to a human after the agent gathers evidence.
