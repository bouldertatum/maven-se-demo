6. **Evidence** — Show Reasoning and Test Suite results.

8. **Objections and close** — trust, accuracy, control, cost, and expansion criteria.
9. **About me** — concrete working style and comfort with ambiguity.
10. **Impactful outcome** — insert a real, measurable result from prior experience.
